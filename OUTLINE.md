**Subtitle:** How to work on 19 different projects simultaneously  
**Runtime:** 5:50 planned delivery + 0:10 live buffer = 6:00 maximum  
**Navigation:** visible arrows, slide counter, keyboard, touch, and presenter mode  
**Persistent UI:** small 6:00 countdown, mute, help, and progress indicator

The chart visuals use the inventory as a private design source, but the public slides stay generic: relative growth, selected project thumbnails, and a few era labels rather than a wall of exact counts.
